---
title: "Uniac"
description: "Describe a system in one file; the platform runs it."
---

Uniac is a cloud deployment platform. You describe a system of container-based
services in one `uniac.yaml`, the `uniac` CLI resolves it into a deployable
artifact, and the platform runs it: it injects each service's resolved
environment, places containers on a private network addressable by service
name, and allocates public addresses for whatever you claim. Nothing of Uniac
ships inside your image — your container is entirely your own.
