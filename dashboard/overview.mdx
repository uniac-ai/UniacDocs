title: "Dashboard"
description: "What you can do at uniac.ai."
---

The web dashboard at [uniac.ai](https://uniac.ai) shows the same per-service
account as [`uniac status`](/cli/overview#uniac-status) — version, status,
endpoints — and covers what the CLI deliberately does not: creating and
deleting projects, retiring services, scaling replicas, and managing your
account and billing.
